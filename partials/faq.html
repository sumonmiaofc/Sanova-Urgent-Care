Urgent care treats non-life-threatening conditions — sprains, infections, fevers, minor cuts, and flu. The ER is for emergencies like chest pain, stroke symptoms, severe bleeding, or major trauma. Urgent care visits are usually faster and cost a fraction of an ER visit.
